Section 1 – 4

Listening

40 questions across 4 sections — social, educational, and academic contexts.

Band 8.0+

Section Breakdown

  • Section 1 Social conversation — 2 speakers, form filling
  • Section 2 Monologue — community context, map/diagram
  • Section 3 Academic discussion — up to 4 speakers
  • Section 4 Academic lecture — hardest section, no pause

Question Types

  • ✅ Multiple Choice (A, B, C)
  • ✅ Matching & Map Labelling
  • ✅ Form / Note / Table Completion
  • ✅ Sentence Completion (≤ 3 words)
  • ✅ Short Answer Questions

🏆 Pro Strategies

  • Predict: Read questions before audio plays
  • Spelling: Practice British spellings (colour, centre)
  • Distractors: Speakers often change their answer — listen till end
  • Plurals: Missing an 's' = wrong answer
  • Numbers: Write numerals not words unless instructed

📝 Note-Taking Template

BEFORE AUDIO:
→ Underline keywords in questions
→ Note expected answer type (name/number/place)

DURING AUDIO:
→ Follow the order — answers are sequential
→ Don't dwell — move to the next question

AFTER AUDIO:
→ 10-min transfer time — check spelling

3 Passages · 40 Questions · 60 Min

Reading

Academic and General Training differ in text complexity and source material.

Band 8.0+

Text Sources

  • 📰 Academic journals & research papers
  • 📰 Books, magazines (scientific/cultural)
  • 📰 Texts written for non-specialist readers
  • 📊 May include graphs, diagrams, illustrations

Question Types

  • ⚡ True / False / Not Given
  • ⚡ Yes / No / Not Given
  • ⚡ Matching Headings to paragraphs
  • ⚡ Matching Information
  • ⚡ Summary / Sentence Completion
  • ⚡ Multiple Choice

True/False/Not Given Tips

TRUE: Statement matches the text exactly

FALSE: Statement contradicts the text

NOT GIVEN: No information about it in the text

⚠️ NOT GIVEN ≠ the statement is wrong. It simply isn't mentioned!

Speed Strategies

  • 🔍 Skim: Read title, first & last sentences (90 sec/passage)
  • 🔍 Scan: Look for keywords from the question
  • 🔍 Paraphrase: Answers use synonyms, not exact words
  • 🔍 Time: Aim 18 min/passage, 6 min to transfer

Section Breakdown

  • Section 1 2–3 short factual texts (ads, notices)
  • Section 2 2 work-related texts (job descriptions)
  • Section 3 1 longer descriptive/instructional text

Key Differences from Academic

  • ✅ More practical, everyday language
  • ✅ Texts from brochures, ads, manuals
  • ✅ Section 3 is more complex — treat like Academic
  • ✅ Band conversion is slightly more lenient

Matching Headings Tips

1. Read all headings first

2. Find the main idea of each paragraph

3. Eliminate wrong ones first

4. Headings reflect the whole paragraph — not just one sentence

GT Band Leniency

For GT Reading, you need ~2–3 more correct answers to achieve the same band as Academic.

Example: Band 7.0 requires 30/40 in Academic vs 34/40 in GT.

Sample Band 8.0 Overview Sentence

✨ Band 8.0 Sample
"Overall, it is clear that the consumption of fast food increased substantially across all three countries over the period shown, with the United States recording the highest figures throughout."
Examiner Note: This overview mentions the overall trend + standout feature without specific data. It avoids being a conclusion — earning full marks for Task Achievement.

BODY 1: First reason supporting your view
          + example/evidence
BODY 2: Second reason + counterargument
          + refutation
CONCLUSION: Restate opinion + summary
            (~250–280 words ideal)

            (~270 words ideal)

Band 8.0 Sample Essay Paragraph — Opinion Essay

✨ Band 8.0 Sample
"One compelling argument in favour of remote work is its profound impact on employee productivity. When individuals are afforded the flexibility to structure their own working environment, they are far more likely to enter a state of deep concentration, free from the incessant interruptions that typify open-plan offices. Examiner Note: This paragraph demonstrates a clear topic sentence, sophisticated vocabulary ('afforded', 'incessant', 'corroborating'), an extended idea, and specific evidence. It would score Band 8.0–8.5 for Lexical Resource.

✨ Band 8.0 Full Essay

Opinion Essay: Remote Work

Topic: Some people think that working from home is beneficial. Others believe it creates more problems than advantages. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

Introduction

In recent decades, advances in digital technology have fundamentally transformed the nature of employment, enabling millions of workers to perform their professional duties from the comfort of their homes. While proponents argue that remote work enhances productivity and work-life balance, critics contend that it undermines collaboration and erodes workplace culture. I firmly believe that, on balance, the advantages of remote working considerably outweigh its drawbacks.

Examiner: Clear thesis, balanced intro, sophisticated vocabulary ('contend', 'erodes'). Band 8.0 introduction.
Body Paragraph 1

One compelling argument in favour of remote work is its profound impact on employee productivity. When individuals are afforded the flexibility to structure their own working environment, they are far more likely to enter a state of deep concentration, free from the incessant interruptions that typify open-plan offices. A study by Stanford University found that remote employees demonstrated a 13% increase in performance, corroborating the notion that autonomy fosters efficiency. Furthermore, the elimination of lengthy commutes allows workers to reclaim considerable time, which can be redirected towards professional development or personal well-being.

Examiner: Extended idea, specific evidence, cohesive devices ('Furthermore'), advanced collocations. Band 8.0–8.5 vocabulary.
Body Paragraph 2

Nevertheless, the concerns surrounding remote work are not without merit. Detractors argue that the absence of face-to-face interaction stifles spontaneous collaboration, which is often the catalyst for innovation. Junior employees, in particular, may suffer from a lack of mentorship opportunities that naturally arise in physical office settings. However, these challenges are increasingly being mitigated by sophisticated virtual collaboration tools such as Slack and Microsoft Teams, which facilitate real-time communication and project management across distributed teams.

Examiner: Counter-argument acknowledged and refuted — demonstrates sophisticated argumentation. Conditional grammar used accurately.
Conclusion

In conclusion, while remote working is not without its challenges, particularly regarding collaboration and mentorship, the substantial benefits it offers in terms of productivity, employee satisfaction, and environmental impact make it a largely positive development. Governments and corporations alike should invest in the infrastructure and policies necessary to support this evolving work paradigm.

Examiner: Restates thesis, summarises main points, ends with forward-looking statement. Clean, concise conclusion. Band 8.0 overall.
